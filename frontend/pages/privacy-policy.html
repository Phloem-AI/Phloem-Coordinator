Privacy Policy

At Phloem AI, we keep privacy simple. We only care about data that helps us connect your agents smoothly and build a better multi-agent orchestration tool. This policy explains how data is handled while we are in our MVP/prototype phase.

1. Zero-Data Retention Principle

Our core architecture is built with privacy in mind. Phloem AI features a Zero-Data Retention pipeline for agent messages. We temporarily process or store communication data strictly while transferring it between your destined agents. Once the message handover is completed, that operational transit data is deleted from our active communication relay.

2. What Data We Collect & Store

  • Account & Authentication Info: We use backend services (like Supabase) to manage accounts. If you sign up, we safely hold basic details like your email and generate a UUID API KEY for agent authorization.
  • Configuration Metadata: To make agent handoffs work, we handle integration parameters, setup schemas, or remote connection metadata.
  • Website Analytics: We use third-party tools to count visitors. See the section below for details.

3. Google Analytics & Tracking Cookies

We use Google Analytics to monitor traffic, count how many users visit the app, and see which sections are popular.

  • Google Analytics collects standard internet log information and visitor behavior data in an anonymous form (such as browser type and pages visited).
  • This tool uses "cookies," which are small text files placed on your computer, to keep track of this data.
  • This data is used strictly to analyze website performance and improve the MVP experience. If you prefer, you can disable cookies through your browser settings.

4. Secrets Management & Credentials

We understand that you link developer environments to Phloem AI. Any third-party API tokens, credentials, or keys passing through our platform are handled with security filtering designed to convey necessary data while separating out confidential information. We do not sell, rent, or trade your credentials.

5. AI Processing & Model Training

We do not use your source code, private agent prompts, or workflow schemas to train baseline AI models. Your engineering data belongs to you.

6. Data Deletion & Your Rights

We want you to have full control over your information. If you want us to completely wipe your account data, configuration settings, or metadata from our database infrastructure, you can request full data deletion at any time.

To request data deletion, simply send us an email using the contact email address listed in the footer of this website. We will process your deletion request promptly.

7. Changes to This Policy

As Phloem AI grows from a prototype into a mature product, this policy might change. Check back here to see updates.
